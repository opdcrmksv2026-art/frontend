# Karan Singh Vaidh (KSV) - Frontend

Enterprise Healthcare & Clinic OPD CRM, Billing & Multi-Entity Inventory Management System.

## Features
- **OPD & Patient Management**: Real-time patient registrations, prescription histories, follow-ups, and referral tracking.
- **Invoice & Billing System**: Custom invoice generation with manual/auto invoice numbering, GST tax calculations, and print-ready receipts.
- **Multi-Entity Inventory Management**:
  - **Kiyava Subathu**: Herbal formulations, raw materials, stock registers, and dedicated Sale & Purchase ledger tabs with 1-click Excel (.csv) export.
  - **Karan Singh Vaidh (KSV)**: Pharmacy store inventory, B2B procurement, and dedicated Sale & Purchase ledger tabs with 1-click Excel (.csv) export.
  - **Maxxi Pharma**: Pharmaceutical formulations and inventory tracking.
- **Live Revenue & Sales Reports**: Period filtering (Today, Yesterday, This Month, All Time) with Sales vs Purchases toggle and combined Excel downloads.

## Tech Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
- **Icons**: Lucide React
